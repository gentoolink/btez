Installation responsibility

EZE-SET products must be installed strictly in accordance with the product instructions supplied with them and with accepted trade practice for pipe support and bedding. The installer is solely responsible for the method, quality, and outcome of the installation, including:

  • Selecting the correct cradle for the pipe size, material, and service.
  • Determining anchor spacing, embedment depth, and the number of supports required for the span and load. Spacing must follow the applicable plumbing code, and in no case exceed 8 ft (2.4 m) between anchors.
  • Confirming that the anchor is fully and squarely driven, and that the cradle is properly seated on the pipe.
  • Setting and verifying final grade before, during, and after backfill.
  • Protecting the installation from damage during backfill, compaction, and subsequent construction activity.
  • Locating existing buried utilities and services before driving any anchor.

EZE-SET accepts no responsibility or liability for any loss, damage, cost, or injury arising from improper, incomplete, or unsuitable installation, from installation contrary to the product instructions, or from any modification, alteration, cutting, heating, or repair of the product.

Determining whether ground conditions at a specific location are suitable, and whether an engineered support is required instead of or in addition to EZE-SET, is the responsibility of the purchaser, installer, or their engineer.
