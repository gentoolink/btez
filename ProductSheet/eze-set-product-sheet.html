The system

Three parts. Zero fuss.

01 Screw anchor

One piece, threaded to the point. Drives straight into soft or uneven ground and stays put — no excavation, no poured base.

02 Adjuster plate

Threads up and down the shaft to set height. Your grade control lives on the anchor itself, so fine-tuning never means re-digging.

03 Pipe cradle

Locks onto the bolt head and grips the line — no sag, no shift, no drift. Interchangeable cradles fit multiple pipe sizes.

How it works

Three steps. One tool.

01

Drive

Screw it in. The threaded auger anchors deep in seconds, even where other supports can’t bite. No digging, no concrete.

02

Set

Drop the pipe into the cradle. It locks onto the bolt head and grips the line — no sag, no shift, no drift.

03

Dial in

Thread the adjuster plate up or down to hit exact grade. Fine-tune anytime, before or after backfill.

On the job

Works the way you do.

Pressure systems

Complete the full piping install, then drill the holes for the “squirt” test and run it. Once testing is done, set the chamber over top — no additional supports, and no zip-tying the pipes to the chamber.

Gravity-feed systems

Set the grade and run your testing. Then set the chambers over top for the final install — the line stays exactly where you dialed it in.

Specifications

At a glance

Pipe capacityFits 1″–4″ pipe — one anchor family across the full range, with interchangeable cradles.

InstallationDirect burial into native soils. Screw-in by hand or driver — no excavation, no concrete, no cribbing.
Height adjustmentThreaded adjuster plate travels the shaft for exact grade; adjustable before or after backfill.
ServiceabilityFully reusable and removable — back it out and reset it. Nothing poured, nothing left behind.
